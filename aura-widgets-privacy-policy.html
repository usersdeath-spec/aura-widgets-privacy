Aura Widgets Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Prism Aura ("Prism Aura", "we", "us", or "our") develops and provides Aura Widgets (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how Aura Widgets accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

1. Information the App accesses

Depending on the features you use and permissions you enable, Aura Widgets may request access to:

  • Calendar information: used by calendar/agenda widgets to display calendar information on your device.
  • Approximate location: used for weather-related functionality to determine information relevant to your area.
  • Notification access: an optional Android notification-listener feature. If you explicitly enable it, the App may receive notification information available through that service for the related functionality.
  • Images and other content selected by you: the App may read Android content URIs when needed for widget imagery or customization.
  • Wallpaper and widget/background functionality: Android system permissions are used to provide these features.

2. Collection and transmission of data

Based on the current version audited for release, Aura Widgets does not operate a Prism Aura account system and does not transmit calendar, location, notification, or selected personal content to Prism Aura servers.

Information accessed through Android permissions is intended to be processed locally on your device for the corresponding feature. We do not sell your personal information or use calendar, location, or notification information for advertising or profiling.

3. Third-party services

Aura Widgets does not intentionally send the above personal or sensitive information to Prism Aura servers. If a future version introduces a third-party service that changes how personal or sensitive data is handled, this Privacy Policy and relevant in-app disclosures will be updated.

4. Permissions and your choices

Android permissions and special access are controlled by Android. You can deny or revoke applicable permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work or may show limited information if the required access is not granted.

5. Data retention and deletion

Prism Aura does not maintain a remote account or remote database of your calendar, location, notification, or personal widget data through Aura Widgets. Locally stored App data remains subject to your device and Android settings. You can remove local App data through Android app settings or by uninstalling Aura Widgets.

6. Data security

We design Aura Widgets to minimize handling of personal and sensitive information. Information processed locally remains protected by the security controls provided by Android and your device.

7. Children's privacy

Aura Widgets is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Prism Aura server account or online database.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page with an updated date.
